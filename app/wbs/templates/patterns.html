eBird checklists

One checklist a day for eBird (ebird.org, Cornell Lab): every bird the camera saw, with the most seen at once in one clip. A bird nobody counted goes in as X (present, not counted); a number from the Review page's How many? or LM Studio goes in as is. Download the file, then in eBird choose Submit, Import data, format eBird Record Format (Extended). Nothing is sent from here. Location, region and protocol are on the Settings page (eBird).

Project FeederWatch count

FeederWatch (November to April) asks, for each species, the most birds you saw at once over two days in a row. Pick the first day; the table is your entry. Nothing is sent anywhere: copy it into FeederWatch yourself. A count typed on the Review page wins over LM Studio's, so check the big ones.
